The Basement Simulator

One representative New Hampshire basement — the wet wall, the equipment train, the floor drain, and a licensed technician. Seven real service calls, sixty-eight timed steps. Run them and you will know what actually happens on site: what gets installed, how long it takes, which tool comes out of the van, and what else gets sold while the technician is standing there. Prefer to read? The full written procedures are below the simulator.

1Pick a service callFrom a free water test to a 2.8-hour softener install — each chip is a real job with real on-site minutes.
2Step through itNext / Previous moves the technician around the basement; the highlight ring shows which equipment he's working, and the tool in his hand changes with the task.
3Or press Auto-playThe call runs itself, step by step, with each step's minutes on the clock and the elapsed-time strip filling at the bottom.
4Watch the moneyEvery scenario shows crew size and revenue — including the $0 free test that decides whether this house becomes a $2,000 or $7,500 customer.

Step sequences are reconstructed from a representative operator’s service catalog, the eighteen-step service call documented in the thesis (§07), and the field-test panel; durations are the modeled on-site minutes behind the capacity model. Equipment layout is a representative Seacoast NH wet wall, not a specific customer’s home.
